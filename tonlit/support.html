Support

Tonlit for iPhone, iOS 17 or later

How does Tonlit read my shade?

Tonlit measures skin colour on the forehead, cheeks, chin, under the eyes and on the neck, and shows where it measured. Your shade is a number on Tonlit's own 15-step scale, from Fair (1) to Rich (15), with an undertone: cool, neutral or warm.

Why is my shade different from photo to photo?

Light, camera and makeup change skin colour in a photo. For the steadiest reading, use a bright, front-facing photo in daylight without a filter. Shade numbers are an editing guide, not a medical or personal colour diagnosis, and they do not match any brand's foundation shades.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Every reading and edit runs on your iPhone. Tonlit has no account and no server, and it never sends your photos anywhere.

A tool doesn't change my photo

Face tools need a face that is clearly visible. Arms needs at least one arm visible from the shoulder. Hair needs visible hair. When a tool can't find what it works on, the photo stays as it is.

How do I save?

Tap Save, then Save to Photos. Tonlit asks only for permission to add photos to your library. You can save the full-size photo or a tone card.
